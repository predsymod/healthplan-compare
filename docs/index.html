Compare Colorado health plans by asking your AI assistant

The cheapest plan in a quiet year is rarely the cheapest in a bad one. This tool gives any AI assistant the Colorado plan data and a short set of questions, so it can show how your household's costs change from a light year to a worst case.

Educational cost estimator for people under 65 buying their own ACA plan in Colorado. It does not recommend a plan and is not medical, financial or insurance advice.

How to use it

  1. Copy the prompt below and paste it into an AI assistant that can call web tools. It was tested with Muse.
  2. Answer a few questions. The assistant asks only what it still needs: who is covered, your ZIP code, your income (optional, used to estimate your premium credit), and how much care you expect.
  3. Read the tradeoffs. You get the best plan under five views of the year, a ranked list, and follow-up comparisons between plans you pick.

Prompt to paste

Connect to the tool server at https://healthplan-compare.vercel.app/mcp (MCP over HTTP: POST JSON-RPC, no login). Call initialize and tools/list, read the server instructions, then use only those tools to help me compare Colorado ACA health plans. Ask me only what the tool says it still needs. Quote dollar figures exactly as the tool returns them, and do not pick a plan for me.

About me: I'm [age], my spouse is [age or none], my kids are [ages or none], and we live in ZIP [ZIP].

The first time, your assistant may ask permission to reach healthplan-compare.vercel.app. Nothing is stored, and no account or key is needed.

Summarised from a real test run in Muse. In that run, every plan figure the assistant quoted matched the tool's output.

The five views of a year

ViewWhat it adds to the yearly premium
Premium onlyNothing
Light yearRoutine care, up to $500 per person of the deductible
Managing a conditionThe plan's standard diabetes-care scenario (about $5,600 of care)
Big yearThe plan's standard having-a-baby scenario (about $12,700 of care)
Worst caseThe plan's out-of-pocket maximum

What it does and doesn't do

  • Prices every standard plan sold in your county
  • Handles spouses and up to three charged children
  • Estimates your 2026 premium tax credit from your income, including Silver cost-sharing reductions
  • Gives exact differences between plans
  • Does not recommend a plan
  • Does not include Colorado state subsidies; the credit is an estimate until you file taxes
  • Does not check doctors, hospitals or drug lists
  • Assumes in-network, covered care

For developers

The tools hold no state: the assistant passes what it has learned each call. They are served as a remote MCP server and as plain REST.

POST https://healthplan-compare.vercel.app/mcp
POST https://healthplan-compare.vercel.app/api/engine/next_question
POST https://healthplan-compare.vercel.app/api/engine/rank_plans
POST https://healthplan-compare.vercel.app/api/engine/compare_plans

Data: CMS State-based Exchange QHP public use file for Colorado, plan year 2026, with ZIP lookup from Census and CMS rating-area tables. It does not reflect state subsidies. Source code and contribution log: github.com/predsymod/healthplan-compare. Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon.
